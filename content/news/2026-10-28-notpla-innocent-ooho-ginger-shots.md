Notpla, the London-based sustainable packaging startup, has announced a partnership with innocent drinks to trial its edible and biodegradable Ooho packaging across innocent's ginger shot range.

The Ooho capsules, made from seaweed extract, are designed to replace single-use plastic sachets and bottles for liquid products. Consumers can either eat the packaging whole or dispose of it, where it will biodegrade in a matter of weeks, comparable to a piece of fruit.

The trial represents one of the highest-profile commercial applications of edible packaging technology to date and sees Notpla move from pilot projects and bespoke activations to a mainstream retail product with one of Europe's most recognisable juice brands.

For innocent, the partnership is part of a broader strategy to reduce the environmental footprint of its packaging portfolio, which has already included moves toward recycled PET and increased recycled content across its bottle range. The company has been exploring alternatives to conventional plastic packaging for several years as part of its commitment to become a more circular business.
