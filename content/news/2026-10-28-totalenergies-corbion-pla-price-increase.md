TotalEnergies Corbion, the PLA joint venture between TotalEnergies and Corbion, has announced a price increase across its Luminy PLA product range, citing sustained cost inflation across its operations.

The increase applies to all Luminy PLA grades and reflects the cumulative impact of rising energy, raw material, and logistics costs on the company's production economics. PLA, or polylactic acid, is a compostable bioplastic produced from fermented plant starch and is one of the most widely adopted bioplastics in global markets.

For converters and brand owners who have built packaging and product lines around PLA, the price increase introduces a cost pressure that will need to be absorbed, passed on, or mitigated through design optimisation. Industry observers note that the move signals growing confidence among bioplastic producers that demand fundamentals are strong enough to support pricing power.

The announcement comes as PLA demand continues to grow globally, driven by regulatory restrictions on conventional single-use plastics and corporate sustainability commitments. TotalEnergies Corbion operates a 75,000-tonne-per-year PLA plant in Rayong, Thailand, and is currently evaluating further capacity expansion.
