Kelpi, the Bristol-based startup developing home-compostable packaging films from seaweed, won two titles at this year's Lloyds British Business Excellence Awards, one of the UK's most visible business award programmes.

The company's films are designed to replace conventional flexible plastic laminates in food and consumer goods packaging, breaking down in a home composting environment rather than requiring industrial composting infrastructure. Kelpi has positioned its materials as a drop-in alternative for brands seeking to remove fossil-based plastic from their packaging portfolios without switching converting lines.

The double win follows a period of expansion for the startup, which has been scaling pilot production and building partnerships with consumer goods brands in the UK and beyond. Industry observers see seaweed-based packaging as one of the more credible near-term alternatives in the flexible film space, since it avoids land use and does not compete with food crops for feedstock.

Kelpi's founders said the awards would support the company's fundraising and commercial conversations as it moves from pilot-scale production toward first commercial volumes.
