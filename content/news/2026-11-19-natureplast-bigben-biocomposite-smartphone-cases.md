Normandy flax fibres and scallop shell powder are finding their way into smartphone accessories: Bigben Connected has launched FORCE ORIGIN, a new range of smartphone cases whose biocomposite formulation was developed by NaturePlast, the Normandy-based bioplastics compounder, and moulded by injection specialist Plastitek.

The FORCE ORIGIN range comes in two biocomposite collections, one built on Normandy flax fibres, the other on powder ground from scallop shells. Both Coproducts are combined with a partially bio-sourced polymer to give the cases their characteristic speckled appearance and a reduced reliance on fossil-based material.

## Formulation as the enabler

NaturePlast's role sits at the materials level: the company compounded the mixtures that incorporate the local coproducts, which were then tested and injection-moulded by Plastitek, Bigben Connected's industrial partner. The NaturePlast-Plastitek-Bigben collaboration spans formulation, testing and transformation, and the resulting cases are certified Origine France Garantie, manufactured entirely in France.

The incorporation of local coproducts does more than shrink the fossil share of the polymer: it gives each collection a visible identity and a traceable provenance story, an argument that matters in consumer accessories where differentiation is largely aesthetic. Coproduct choice and loading influence both the appearance and the properties of the final formulation, a balance NaturePlast develops case by case.

## Circular design beyond the material

The range extends the circularity argument beyond the material itself: Bigben Connected plans to collect used cases through its La Recyclerie programme, shred them and feed the recovered material into the production of new cases. For NaturePlast, the application demonstrates how formulation expertise can open new outlets for local coproducts in consumer products, and the company positions the project as a template for integrating agricultural and marine coproducts into everyday goods.
