HELSINKI — Neste Corporation (Nasdaq Helsinki: NESTE) has published its financial reporting schedule for 2027, providing transparency for investors and stakeholders as the company continues to expand its renewables production capacity toward 6.8 million tons annually.

The world's leading producer of renewable diesel and sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) will publish its financial reports in 2027 as follows:

- Financial Statements for fiscal year 2026: 10 February 2027
- Interim Report January–March 2027: 28 April 2027
- Half Year Financial Report January–June 2027: 22 July 2027
- Interim Report January–September 2027: 28 October 2027

The Annual Report 2026 will be published on Neste's website by 2 March 2027 at the latest. The Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, 23 March 2027.

Neste's renewables production capacity is expected to reach 6.8 million tons annually in 2027, with production on three continents. The company also produces high-quality oil products at its Porvoo refinery in Finland and operates a network of nearly 1,000 fuel stations with expanding service offerings, including EV charging, in Finland and the Baltics.

Neste's strategy focuses on growth in renewable fuels, which help customers reduce their greenhouse gas emissions. The company is included in many international indices for its sustainability performance. In 2025, Neste's revenue stood at EUR 19.0 billion.
