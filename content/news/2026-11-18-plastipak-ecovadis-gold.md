Plastipak has earned an EcoVadis Gold medal, with the sustainability assessment platform noting a 12-point increase in the packaging producer's overall score. The rating covers Plastipak's environmental, labour and human rights, ethics and sustainable procurement performance.

EcoVadis ratings are widely used by brand owners and retailers to screen their packaging suppliers, and a Gold medal places a supplier in a leading band of assessed companies. For Plastipak, whose business spans PET packaging production and one of the largest PET recycling operations in the world through its Clean Tech subsidiary, the score increase reflects the operational significance of its closed-loop recycling model, in which bottles are collected, processed into recycled PET and returned into new food-grade packaging.

## Certification as commercial currency

Sustainability ratings have become commercial currency in packaging supply chains: brand owners increasingly cascade their own climate and circularity commitments down to packaging suppliers, and EcoVadis scorecards are among the most common screening tools in that cascade. A Gold rating with a double-digit score increase strengthens a packaging producer's position in tenders where brand owners carry public commitments on recycled content and emissions.

The recognition follows the opening of Plastipak's new PET material development centre in Verbania, Italy, which strengthens the company's European PET recycling and material development capabilities. Plastipak positions the two moves as parts of one strategy: material development capacity on the one hand, and third-party-validated sustainability performance on the other, both aimed at brand owners who now specify recycled content and carbon performance in their packaging tenders.
