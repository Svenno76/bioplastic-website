Swedish specialty chemicals company Nexam Chemical has received its largest single order to date for its reactive recycling additives, a contract worth approximately SEK 5 million.

## Additives that repair polymer chains

Nexam's additives work by reconnecting polymer chains that mechanical recycling breaks — restoring molecular weight and thus mechanical properties in recycled PET and polyolefins. The chemistry, sometimes described as reactive recycling or chain re-extension, addresses the core quality problem of mechanical recycling: each pass through the process shortens polymer chains, degrading performance until the recyclate can only be downcycled.

## A reference order

The order's size marks the transition from pilot-scale trials to volume supply. For a specialty chemicals company of Nexam's scale, the contract suggests a customer scaling up recycled-content production, likely against European recycled-content mandates that tighten through the decade under the EU's packaging regulation.

Nexam has stated that the order will be delivered over the coming quarters, with revenue recognised progressively.
