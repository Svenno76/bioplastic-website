Mitsui Chemicals has signed a share purchase agreement for the acquisition of JSR Corporation, the Japanese synthetic rubber and specialty materials company, in a transaction with significant implications for the structure of Japan's materials industry.

JSR, which was taken private by JIC Capital in a deal completed in 2024, is a global leader in semiconductor photoresist materials and also maintains substantial businesses in synthetic rubbers, specialty polymers, and life sciences materials. The company's semiconductor materials business, in particular, has become strategically important as demand for advanced chip manufacturing materials continues to grow.

For Mitsui Chemicals, the acquisition represents a significant acceleration of its strategy to shift its portfolio away from commodity petrochemicals and toward higher-value specialty materials. The company has been progressively restructuring its business, closing or consolidating commodity petrochemical capacity while investing in performance materials, functional polymers, and sustainable chemistry platforms.

The transaction is subject to regulatory approvals in multiple jurisdictions and is expected to close over the coming months, subject to satisfaction of customary closing conditions.
