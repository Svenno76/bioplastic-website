European Bioplastics, the industry association for biobased and biodegradable plastics, has welcomed the publication of new European technical specifications for packaging design-for-recycling, describing them as a practical and harmonised framework for assessing packaging circularity across the EU single market.

The specifications, developed by the European Committee for Standardization (CEN), provide a common methodology for evaluating how packaging design choices affect recyclability outcomes across different material streams. For bioplastics, the framework offers a structured approach to assessing compostable packaging formats, which have often been assessed inconsistently across different member states.

The association noted that harmonised design-for-recycling standards are particularly significant for biodegradable and compostable materials, which have historically faced a fragmented regulatory landscape across European markets. A common framework allows producers to design packaging in confidence, knowing that a single design can meet circularity requirements across multiple member states.

European Bioplastics called on member states to adopt the specifications into their national frameworks and to avoid divergent national approaches that would fragment the single market for sustainable packaging.
