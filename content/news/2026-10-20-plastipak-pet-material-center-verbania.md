Plastipak, one of Europe's largest producers of PET packaging for the food and beverage sector, has opened a new PET material development centre in Verbania, Italy, strengthening its research and development capabilities in the region.

The facility will focus on advancing the company's PET recycling and material science capabilities, supporting the development of new packaging solutions that meet increasingly stringent European regulatory requirements for recyclability and recycled content.

Plastipak has been investing significantly in its European recycling infrastructure in recent years, operating as both one of the continent's largest PET converters and one of its largest PET recyclers. The company's Close the Loop recycling division processes used PET bottles into food-grade recycled PET, which is then used in new bottle production.

The Verbania facility will serve as a hub for material innovation, supporting Plastipak's work with brand owners on packaging that meets both regulatory requirements and consumer expectations for sustainability.
