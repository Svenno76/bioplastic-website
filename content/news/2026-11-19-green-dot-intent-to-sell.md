Green Dot Bioplastics, a Kansas-based compounder of biobased and compostable plastics, has announced its intent to sell the company. The announcement positions the formulator behind the Terratek brand as one of the first established US bioplastics specialists to formally go to market in the current cycle.

The company supplies biobased compounds, compostable resins, and hybrid materials that blend renewable fillers with conventional polymers, serving packaging, consumer goods, and durable applications from its Emerald, Kansas, base.

## A test of buyer appetite

A sale would mark a consolidation point for the North American bioplastics compounding segment, which remains fragmented among a handful of independent formulators alongside the large resin producers. Strategic acquirers from the conventional plastics and packaging sectors have historically been the most active buyers in this space, drawn by established customer bases and qualified formulations that would take years to replicate.

The announcement gives no timeline for the process and no indication of the preferred buyer profile.
