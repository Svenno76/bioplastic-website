Agilyx, the Norwegian-American chemical recycling company, has strengthened its position in the European circular plastics value chain through the acquisition of French extended producer responsibility scheme Léko by its subsidiary GreenDot.

Léko operates as an approved producer responsibility organisation in France, managing the collection and recycling obligations of branded goods producers under the country's extended producer responsibility framework. The scheme has built a significant membership base among French brands and retailers since its founding.

The acquisition gives Agilyx direct access to waste stream management infrastructure and producer relationships in Europe's second-largest economy, complementing the company's chemical recycling technology portfolio. It also positions GreenDot as an integrated player capable of managing the full loop from collection through to the production of recycled feedstock for new plastic production.

The move comes as the European Union continues to tighten requirements around plastic packaging recycling and recycled content, driving demand for both physical recycled material and the infrastructure needed to produce it at scale.
