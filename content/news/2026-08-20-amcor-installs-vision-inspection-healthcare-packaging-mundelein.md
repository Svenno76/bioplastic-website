DEERFIELD, Ill. — Amcor (NYSE: AMCR, ASX: AMC), a global leader in developing and producing responsible packaging solutions, has announced the installation of state-of-the-art automated manufacturing lines and vision inspection systems for healthcare bag production at its Mundelein, Illinois facility. The investment represents a significant advancement in packaging reliability and safety for the medical and healthcare sectors.

Medical product recalls due to sterile barrier breach are commonly connected to packaging seal failures, pin holes, and contamination. Amcor's new state-of-the-art vision systems inspect 100 percent of the bags produced, including film-to-film and film-to-Tyvek® seals, delivering exceptional confidence in package quality. Production data is serialized to individual bags, providing full manufacturing traceability from raw material to finished package.

As part of Amcor's broad global healthcare packaging network, the Mundelein facility delivers robust vertical integration with full control of its blown film, printing, laminating, slitting, and converting processes. This end-to-end control allows for optimal service, efficiency, and product consistency. Vertical integration, coupled with superior quality inspection, reduces risks and costly packaging failures for healthcare manufacturers.

"The investment in our Mundelein, Ill. facility underscores Amcor's commitment to providing unmatched quality, service and supply chain security to our healthcare customers," said Art Castro, Vice President of Amcor Flexibles Healthcare in North America. "The installation of cutting-edge equipment reflects Amcor's continued leadership in the healthcare industry."

Amcor's healthcare packaging portfolio includes flexible packaging, rigid packaging, cartons, and closures designed for nutrition, health, beauty, and wellness categories. The company's global product innovation and sustainability expertise enables it to solve packaging challenges around the world, producing packaging that is more sustainable, functional, and appealing for customers and their consumers.

The Mundelein facility's automation upgrade comes at a time when healthcare packaging demands are intensifying. Regulatory requirements for sterile barrier integrity, traceability, and patient safety are becoming more stringent globally. Amcor's approach — combining vertical integration with 100% automated inspection — addresses these requirements at the source rather than relying on sampling-based quality control.

The vision inspection technology uses high-resolution cameras and advanced algorithms to detect defects at micron-level resolution across every bag produced. When a defect is identified, the system automatically rejects the affected package and captures production data for continuous improvement. This level of inspection coverage and traceability is particularly critical for applications where packaging failure could compromise sterile medical devices, pharmaceuticals, or diagnostic products.

Tyvek®, a registered trademark of DuPont de Nemours, Inc., is widely used in medical packaging for its microbial barrier properties and compatibility with various sterilization methods. The film-to-Tyvek seal inspection capability addresses one of the most challenging quality control points in healthcare packaging, where seal integrity directly impacts sterility assurance.

Amcor's purpose of elevating customers, shaping lives, and protecting the future is supported by a commitment to safety across its operations. Over 75,000 people generate $23 billion in annualized sales from operations that span over 400 locations in more than 40 countries. The company's sustainability strategy encompasses circularity, product innovation, and responsible packaging solutions across its portfolio.

The Mundelein investment aligns with broader industry trends toward automation and digitalization in medical manufacturing. As healthcare systems demand higher reliability and traceability from their packaging suppliers, investments in advanced inspection and serialization technology are becoming a competitive differentiator.
