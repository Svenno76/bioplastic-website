Avantium and Will & Co B.V., a distributor of specialty and bio-based chemicals, have taken the next step in their collaboration by signing a capacity reservation for FDCA (furandicarboxylic acid) from future licensed production capacity. The agreement reflects Will & Co's interest in securing future access to FDCA for applications in the coatings, adhesives, sealants and elastomers industries.

FDCA is a versatile bio-based building block used in synthetic materials including alkyd resins, surfactants and polyester polyols, with potential applications ranging from paints and adhesives to flooring, tubing, automotive components and construction materials. The reservation builds on a collaboration the two companies announced in February 2026 to accelerate the development and adoption of FDCA in those markets.

## De-risking future capacity

Under the agreement, Will & Co has expressed its interest in reserving future FDCA capacity from plants operated by licensees of Avantium's YXY Technology, which catalytically converts plant-based sugars into FDCA, the key building block for the plant-based polymer PEF. Avantium intends to introduce Will & Co to prospective licensees and facilitate discussions on securing capacity, subject to final agreement between the parties.

For prospective licensees, capacity reservations provide greater visibility of demand for the output of future plants. By building commercial demand ahead of new production capacity, Avantium aims to de-risk the investment case for licensees. "This capacity reservation shows how Avantium's collaboration with Will & Co is progressing from application development toward commercialization," said Marco Jansen, chief commercial officer at Avantium.

Jacques van Lindonk, managing director at Will & Co, said reserving future capacity was "a logical next step as we work towards bringing FDCA-based solutions to our customers and the wider market." The two companies will continue joint development activities in parallel, with Avantium providing FDCA and technical support for application work.
