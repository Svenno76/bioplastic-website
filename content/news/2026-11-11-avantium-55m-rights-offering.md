Avantium has announced the successful completion of its fully committed and underwritten rights offering, raising gross proceeds of approximately EUR 55.45 million. The offering resulted in the issue of 33,608,956 new ordinary shares at an issue price of EUR 1.65.

By the close of the exercise period on 10 November 2026, 28,817,796 offer shares had been subscribed through the exercise of rights, a take-up rate of 85.7 percent. The rump placement of the remaining shares was fully subscribed.

## Funding the flagship ramp-up

The company says the financing provides the funds to execute the next phase of its strategy, including the ramp-up of the FDCA Flagship Plant in Delfzijl, the world's first commercial-scale FDCA production facility, where the first FDCA has already been produced. The funds also cover preparation of the plant's first commercial deliveries and further development of Avantium's licensing business, through which it plans to deploy FDCA production capacity globally.

"With the first FDCA successfully produced at our Flagship Plant and this financing secured, our focus is firmly on what comes next: preparing for first commercial product deliveries and accelerating our commercial and licensing activities," said Tom van Aken, chief executive officer of Avantium.

The raise is the second capital infusion around the Dutch polymer company in recent weeks, following a capacity reservation agreement with distributor Will & Co announced the next day, and underlines the financial groundwork the company considers necessary to scale FDCA and PEF from demonstration to commercial volumes. Avantium's shares trade on Euronext Amsterdam and Brussels under the symbol AVTX.
