Kingfa Sci. and Tech. Co. Ltd., the Chinese polymer compounding specialist, has committed USD 50 million to expand its production facility in San Luis Potosí, Mexico, strengthening its ability to serve North American customers from within the region.

The investment will increase the site's compounding capacity and add new production lines, supporting Kingfa's growing customer base in the Mexican market and across North America under the USMCA trade framework. San Luis Potosí has become a strategic location for automotive and industrial suppliers serving both the Mexican domestic market and export customers across North America.

For Kingfa, the expansion reflects the broader trend of supply chain regionalisation, with customers increasingly requiring local production of engineered polymer compounds for automotive, electrical, and consumer applications. The company supplies a range of modified engineering plastics, including glass-fibre reinforced grades, flame-retardant compounds, and sustainable material lines.

The announcement underscores Mexico's growing importance as a manufacturing hub for the North American plastics and polymer industry.
