Teknor Apex, the US polymer compounding specialist, has announced the appointment of a new Chief Technology Officer, tasking the role with accelerating innovation across the company's broad compound portfolio.

The company, which operates divisions spanning vinyl compounds, engineered plastics, thermoplastic elastomers, and biopolymers, has been investing in sustainable material solutions across all of these product areas. Its biopolymer division has developed a range of bio-based and compostable compound solutions for packaging, consumer goods, and agricultural applications.

The appointment comes at a critical time for the specialty compounds industry, as customers across sectors demand materials that combine technical performance with demonstrated sustainability credentials. Compounders are increasingly expected to offer solutions that address recyclability, bio-based content, and carbon footprint, while maintaining the performance characteristics that have made conventional compounds the default choice in so many applications.

Teknor Apex said the new CTO will lead the company's efforts to expand its sustainable material solutions while continuing to serve the demanding technical requirements of its core markets.
