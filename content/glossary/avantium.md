**Avantium N.V.** is a Dutch renewable chemistry company headquartered in Amsterdam, Netherlands. It develops and commercializes catalytic technologies that convert plant-based sugars into fully bio-based chemicals and polymers, with its flagship products being **FDCA (2,5-furandicarboxylic acid)** and **PEF (polyethylene furanoate)** — a 100% plant-based polyester marketed under the **releaf®** brand.

## Technology Platform

Avantium's core technology is the **YXY® catalytic process**, which dehydrates plant-based sugars into HMF (hydroxymethylfurfural) and then oxidizes HMF into FDCA. FDCA is polymerized with monoethylene glycol to produce PEF, a polyester with a furan ring in place of PET's benzene ring. The furan chemistry gives PEF measurably better oxygen and carbon dioxide barrier properties than PET, along with higher glass transition temperature and mechanical strength — advantages for beverage bottles, films, and textiles.

## Commercial Activities

Beyond YXY®, Avantium has developed a portfolio of renewable chemistry ventures:

- **Volta Technology** — electrochemical conversion of CO2 into renewable oxalic acid and oxalates (a carbon-negative route to glycolic acid and other C2 chemicals).
- **Dawn Technology™** — fractionation of low-cost non-food biomass, such as agricultural residues, into sugars and lignin for downstream uses.
- **Licensing and partnerships** — Avantium works with brand owners and packaging converters (including Coca-Cola, Danone, and [Gepack](/companies/gepack/)) and reserves capacity with fillers such as Hoogesteger ahead of the commercial-scale FDCA plant in Delfzijl, Netherlands.

The company is listed on Euronext Amsterdam (ticker AVTX) and operates a pilot plant in Geleen alongside demonstration-scale FDCA capacity in Delfzijl, targeting commercial FDCA production as a key milestone in its scale-up roadmap.

## Significance

Avantium is one of the few companies worldwide that has taken a novel bio-based polyester from laboratory chemistry through pilot and demonstration plants toward commercial scale, making it a reference case for how new sustainable polymers reach the market.
