## Overview

**YXY® Technology** is Avantium's patented catalytic process for producing **FDCA (2,5-furandicarboxylic acid)** from plant-based sugars. FDCA is the key monomer building block for **PEF (polyethylene furanoate)** — Avantium's flagship biopolymer marketed under the **releaf®** brand.

The technology name derives from the chemical transformation: **Xylose → (Y) → FDCA** (where Y represents the catalytic conversion steps).

## Process Chemistry

### Feedstock
- **Primary**: Fructose from starch crops (corn, wheat, potatoes)
- **Future**: 2nd-generation sugars from lignocellulosic biomass (agricultural residues, forestry waste)
- **Sustainability**: Renewable, non-food competing feedstocks under development

### Reaction Steps

| Step | Transformation | Key Innovation |
|------|----------------|----------------|
| 1. Dehydration | Fructose → HMF (hydroxymethylfurfural) | Selective acid catalysis |
| 2. Oxidation | HMF → FDCA | Catalytic oxidation with high selectivity |
| 3. Purification | Crude FDCA → Polymer-grade FDCA | Proprietary separation technology |

### YXY® Catalytic System
- **Proprietary catalyst formulations** for both dehydration and oxidation steps
- **High selectivity** to FDCA (>90% yield from fructose)
- **Mild conditions** compared to traditional petrochemical routes
- **Continuous process** design for commercial scale

## Key Advantages vs. Alternative Routes

| Aspect | YXY® (Avantium) | Traditional TPA Route |
|--------|-----------------|----------------------|
| **Feedstock** | Renewable sugars | Crude oil (paraxylene) |
| **Steps** | 2 catalytic steps | Multi-step oxidation |
| **Selectivity** | >90% to FDCA | ~65-75% to TPA |
| **Energy** | Lower temperature | High-temp oxidation |
| **Byproducts** | Minimal | Significant |
| **Carbon footprint** | ~70% lower | Baseline |

## Applications Enabled

YXY® Technology enables production of:
1. **FDCA** — Monomer for PEF (polyethylene furanoate)
2. **PEF (releaf®)** — 100% bio-based PET alternative with superior barriers
3. **Polyesters** — PBF, PTF, and other furanic polyesters
4. **Coatings & Resins** — Furanic building blocks for specialty applications
5. **Plasticizers** — Bio-based alternatives to phthalates
