**Gepack** is a Portuguese packaging manufacturer specializing in PET and innovative polymer packaging for the food, beverage, and consumer goods industries. The company produces bottles and containers on injection stretch blow molding lines and works with both conventional and next-generation polymers.

## Role in the PEF Value Chain

Gepack gained prominence in the bioplastics sector through its collaboration with [Avantium](/companies/avantium/), the Dutch renewable chemistry company behind **releaf®** PEF (polyethylene furanoate). In this partnership, Gepack handles the conversion side of the value chain:

- **Processing a novel polymer** — PEF has different melt-flow and crystallization behavior than PET, so converting it commercially required adapting blow molding process parameters.
- **Enabling brand launches** — Gepack's bottles made it possible for beverage brands such as [Frubaça](/companies/frubaca/) to package products in 100% plant-based PEF.
- **Validating recyclability** — PEF is designed to be recyclable in the existing PET recycling stream, and converter experience is central to demonstrating this in practice.

## Significance

Gepack illustrates the role of small and mid-sized converters in commercializing new biopolymers: resin producers can only reach the market when converters are willing to run new materials on production equipment. Its early PEF work makes it one of the first European converters with hands-on industrial experience in processing furan-based polyesters.
