**Evonik Industries AG** is a German specialty chemicals company headquartered in Essen, Germany. Within the bioplastics and circular-economy field, Evonik is active on several fronts: it produces bio-based and biodegradable specialty polymers, develops additives that make conventional plastics easier to recycle, and supplies catalyst and adsorbent technology for **chemical recycling** of plastic waste.

## Circular-Economy Portfolio

- **Purocel™** — a portfolio of hydrotreating catalysts and fixed-bed adsorbents that purify pyrolysis oil from mixed plastic waste into steam-cracker-ready feedstock, addressing one of the main bottlenecks of chemical recycling (see [Purocel™](/glossary/purocel/)).
- **VESTENAMER®** — a process additive that improves the mechanical recycling of rubber and polymer compounds.
- **TEGO® Cycle** — additive solutions that enable the recyclability of coatings and adhesives on packaging.
- **DEGACRYL®** — recyclable adhesive systems for packaging applications.
- **Bio-based and biodegradable polymers** — specialty polyesters and other polymers derived from renewable feedstocks for medical, personal care, and industrial uses.

## Business Model

Evonik is primarily a **B2B supplier** rather than a polymer resin producer at commodity scale: its role in the bioplastics value chain is to supply the specialty ingredients, catalysts, and process technologies that other producers, converters, and recyclers need. This positions the company across the whole circular-plastics value chain — from making virgin production more sustainable to enabling end-of-life recycling.

## Market Context

Evonik is one of many large chemical companies responding to the EU's **Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR)** recycled-content mandates and to brand-owner commitments on circular packaging. Its catalyst technology for pyrolysis-oil purification is part of a broader industry effort to make chemical recycling of mixed plastic waste technically and economically viable at scale.
