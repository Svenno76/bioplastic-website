**Zeopore Technologies NV** is a Belgian catalyst company, spun out of KU Leuven, that specializes in improving **zeolite catalysts** — microporous aluminosilicate materials widely used in refining and petrochemistry.

## Mesoporous Zeolite Technology

Conventional zeolites have extremely small pores (well under 1 nanometer) that exclude bulky molecules, which limits their use in processing heavy or contaminated feeds. Zeopore's core innovation is a set of patented **post-synthetic modification processes** that introduce controlled **mesopores** (2–50 nm) into zeolite crystals without destroying their crystalline structure or acidity. The result is a catalyst that combines:

- **Shape-selective chemistry** — the catalytic precision of the original micropores.
- **Open transport highways** — mesopores that let bulky molecules such as plastic-waste-derived pyrolysis oils reach the active sites quickly.
- **Tuned acidity** — chemical functionality adjusted to each application, from cracking to deoxygenation.

## Relevance to Chemical Recycling

In plastic-waste pyrolysis, the oil produced from mixed plastic waste is often too contaminated or too aromatic to feed directly into steam crackers. Zeopore's mesoporous catalysts are being applied to **upgrade pyrolysis oil** — removing heteroatoms, cracking heavy fractions, and steering product distribution toward cracker-ready, drop-in hydrocarbon streams — a key enabling step for scaling chemical recycling of plastic waste in Europe.
