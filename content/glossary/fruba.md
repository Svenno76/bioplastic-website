**Frubaça** is a Portuguese juice and beverage producer based in Vila Nova de Gaia in the Porto region, specializing in fruit juices, functional beverages, and innovative drink formats. The company is best known in the bioplastics world as one of the first beverage brands to bring bottles made of **PEF (polyethylene furanoate)** to market.

## PEF Packaging Pioneer

Frubaça packages a functional beverage shot in bottles made from Avantium's **releaf®** PEF, produced in collaboration with Portuguese packaging manufacturer **[Gepack](/companies/gepack/)**. The project is a landmark commercial use of PEF because:

- **100% plant-based bottle** — PEF is derived from plant-based sugars via Avantium's YXY® technology, rather than from fossil naphtha.
- **Superior barrier properties** — PEF's oxygen and CO2 barrier is roughly an order of magnitude better than PET, extending the shelf life of oxygen-sensitive juice products.
- **Existing infrastructure** — PEF bottles are processed on standard injection stretch blow molding equipment and are designed to be recyclable in the PET stream.

## Significance

For the bioplastics industry, Frubaça's launch is frequently cited as evidence that next-generation bio-based polymers can move from pilot chemistry to real consumer products. It demonstrates a complete European PEF value chain: [Avantium](/companies/avantium/) supplies the polymer, [Gepack](/companies/gepack/) converts it into bottles, and Frubaça fills and markets them.
