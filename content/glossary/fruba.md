# Frubaca

**Frubaca** is a Portuguese juice and beverage producer based in Vila Nova de Gaia (Porto region), specializing in fruit juices, functional beverages, and innovative drink formats. The company is a pioneer in adopting **PEF (releaf®) bottles** for functional beverage shots through its collaboration with Avantium and Gepack.
