## Event Overview

The Plastics Recycling Conference is the largest and most established meeting for the North American plastics recycling industry, organised by the Recycling & Sustainability team behind Resource Recycling and Plastics Recycling World magazines. The 2027 edition runs March 1-3 at the Gaylord National Resort & Convention Center in National Harbor, Maryland, in the Washington, DC area, co-located with the Resource Recycling Conference — both share the venue and trade show floor while running separate conference tracks.

The event combines market-focused sessions with a trade show and extensive networking, and is the annual barometer for North American recycled-plastics supply, demand and policy.

## Key Details

- **Event Type**: Conference with trade show floor
- **Date**: March 1-3, 2027
- **Location**: Gaylord National Resort & Convention Center, National Harbor, Maryland, United States
- **Organizer**: Resource Recycling, Inc.
- **Website**: [Plastics Recycling Conference](https://www.plasticsrecycling.org/)
- **Expected Attendance**: 2,000+ professionals from across the value chain

## Topics & Focus Areas

- **Recycled-Content Demand**: Brand commitments, PCR supply contracts and pricing
- **Regulation & EPR**: State-level extended producer responsibility laws in the US and Canada
- **Recycling Technology**: Sorting, washing, and reprocessing advances, plus chemical recycling
- **Market Intelligence**: Recovered plastics pricing and export dynamics
- **Textiles & Film**: Emerging recycling streams for textiles, film and flexibles
- **Infrastructure Investment**: MRF modernization and reprocessor capacity build-out

## Why Attend

- **Recyclers**: Gauge North American demand, pricing and capacity trends for the year ahead
- **Brand Buyers**: Meet PCR suppliers and understand evolving quality/traceability standards
- **Equipment Suppliers**: Access the densest concentration of North American recycling decision-makers
- **Policy Teams**: Track the US state EPR roll-outs shaping producer obligations

## Typical Attendance

The conference attracts 2,000+ professionals: plastics recyclers, MRF operators, brokers, brand-owner packaging teams, equipment manufacturers, polymer traders, consultants, investors and regulators from the United States, Canada, Latin America and beyond.
