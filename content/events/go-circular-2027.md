## Event Overview

Go Circular 2027 is a two-day conference on the transition from linear to circular plastics, held in Antwerp — heart of Europe's largest petrochemical cluster. The event brings together circular-economy decision-makers from across the plastics value chain to examine the regulatory drivers, economic conditions and technological advances needed for the industry's circular transition.

Sessions address the practical questions facing polymer producers, brand owners, recyclers and chemical companies: how policy (PPWR, EPR schemes, recycled-content targets) reshapes demand, how mechanical and chemical recycling capacity is scaling in Europe, and which business models survive contact with real recycled-material prices.

## Topics & Focus Areas

- **Regulation**: EU circular-economy legislation and its impact on polymer producers and importers
- **Recycled Content**: Meeting PPWR targets — quality, supply and pricing of recyclates
- **Chemical Recycling**: Mass balance, tracking and the contested technology mix
- **Design for Circularity**: Product and packaging design choices that enable recycling at scale
- **Market Economics**: Business models that hold up when virgin and recycled prices diverge

## Why Attend

- **Polymer Producers**: Understand portfolio strategies for the circular transition
- **Brand Owners & Convertors**: Benchmark circularity commitments against regulation and supply reality
- **Recyclers & Technology Suppliers**: Position solutions with Europe's polymer establishment
- **Policymakers & Analysts**: Track how the EU's largest polymer cluster is adapting

## Typical Attendance

The conference draws a mixed senior audience of around 150-250: petrochemical and polymer producers, plastic converters, brand owners, recyclers, technology providers, consultancies and policy specialists active in the Benelux chemical cluster and across Europe.
