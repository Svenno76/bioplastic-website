## Event Overview

COMPOST2027 is the US Composting Council's 35th annual conference and trade show, held at the Prime F. Osborn III Convention Center in Jacksonville, Florida under the theme "Deep Roots, Bright Future: Supporting Compost for 35 Years". It is the largest gathering of the composting industry in North America, combining education sessions, facility tours, and an exhibit hall with the newest composting equipment and technology.

For the bioplastics value chain, COMPOST is the key North American meeting point on the end-of-life side: compostable product standards, certifications, contamination reduction and compost-operator acceptance of foodware and packaging are all core conference themes.

## Topics & Focus Areas

- **Compostable Products**: Standards, certification and compost-operator acceptance of compostable packaging and foodware
- **Contamination Reduction**: Managing look-alike plastics and improving feedstock quality at composting facilities
- **Organics Collection**: Programs, infrastructure and policy for diverting food scraps and compostables
- **Compost Science & Operators**: Process control, product quality and market development
- **Policy & Regulations**: State organics-diversion laws, labelling rules and extended producer responsibility
- **Soil Health & Markets**: Compost end-market development for agriculture, landscaping and restoration

## Why Attend

- **Compostable-Producers**: Meet the operators and municipalities who actually process compostable packaging
- **Brand Owners**: Understand real-world compostability acceptance before making packaging claims
- **Certifiers & Labs**: Connect with the North American organics recycling community
- **Policy & Municipal Teams**: Learn from state-level organics diversion programs

## Typical Attendance

COMPOST2027 draws over 1,500 professionals: compost facility operators, organics-recycling program managers, municipal solid-waste planners, compostable-product makers, equipment manufacturers, researchers, consultants and policy staff.
