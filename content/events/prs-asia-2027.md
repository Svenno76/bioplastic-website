## Event Overview

PRS Asia 2027 is the Plastics Recycling Show for China and South East Asia, bringing the established PRS exhibition-and-conference format to Guangzhou — at the centre of the Pearl River Delta manufacturing belt. The event connects the region's plastics recycling industry: recyclers, brand owners, machinery suppliers, policymakers and sustainability experts.

The exhibition is paired with a conference addressing plastics recycling, circular economy and market conditions in China and South East Asia. PRS Asia is organised by Crain Communications in partnership with Plastics Recyclers Europe, alongside sister shows PRSE (Amsterdam), PRS Brazil (São Paulo) and PRS ME&A (Dubai).

## Topics & Focus Areas

- **Recycled Polymer Supply**: rPET, rPE, rPP and engineered recyclates from Asian reprocessors
- **Machinery & Technology**: Sorting, washing and reprocessing lines for high-volume Asian operations
- **Trade Flows**: Regional and global movement of scrap and recyclates under changing import rules
- **Brand Supply Chains**: Sourcing recycled content for Asian and export manufacturing
- **Policy**: Chinese and ASEAN recycling regulation and circular-economy programs

## Why Attend

- **Recyclers**: Meet Asian and international buyers in the world's largest plastics-processing region
- **Brand Owners**: Map recycled-content supply chains serving Asian manufacturing hubs
- **Machinery Suppliers**: Exhibit to high-volume Asian reprocessors
- **Traders & Analysts**: Track scrap and recyclate flows in the most dynamic regional market

## Typical Attendance

The show serves the regional recycling value chain: plastics recyclers, waste-management and collection companies, machinery suppliers, brand owners, convertors, traders, regulators and industry associations from China, South East Asia and international markets.
