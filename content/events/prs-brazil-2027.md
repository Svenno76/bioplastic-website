## Event Overview

PRS Brazil 2027 is the first edition of the Plastics Recycling Show in Brazil, bringing the international PRS format to Latin America's largest plastics market. The event takes place at the Renaissance São Paulo Hotel and brings together representatives from across the value chain to discuss the challenges, opportunities and pathways for the development of plastics recycling and the circular economy in Brazil and Latin America.

The exhibition is paired with a conference addressing plastics recycling, circular economy, technology, market conditions and regulation in the region. PRS Brazil is organised by Crain Communications and ABIPLAST, in partnership with Plastics Recyclers Europe — the same team behind PRSE in Amsterdam.

## Topics & Focus Areas

- **Recycling Technology**: Sorting, washing and reprocessing equipment for the Latin American market
- **Recycled Materials**: Polymer recyclates and compounds for regional manufacturing supply chains
- **Regulation & EPR**: Extended producer responsibility and recycled-content policy across Latin America
- **Market Intelligence**: Supply, demand and pricing dynamics for recycled plastics in Brazil
- **Circular Economy Business Models**: Collection systems, reverse logistics and partnerships

## Why Attend

- **Recyclers**: Enter or expand in the largest plastics recycling market in Latin America
- **Brand Owners**: Meet regional recyclate suppliers ahead of tightened packaging rules
- **Machinery Suppliers**: Reach Brazilian reprocessors evaluating new technology
- **Policy & Industry Bodies**: Shape the agenda of a brand-new regional industry platform

## Typical Attendance

The inaugural edition targets the full regional value chain: plastics recyclers, waste-management companies, brand owners and convertors, machinery suppliers, polymer producers, regulators, associations, investors and consultants active in Brazil and neighboring markets.
