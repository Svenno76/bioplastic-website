## Event Overview

Fakuma 2027 is the 31st edition of the international trade fair for plastics processing, held at Messe Friedrichshafen on Lake Constance. Organized by P. E. Schall, the biennial fair is one of Europe's most important plastics-industry events, with focus on injection molding, extrusion, thermoforming and 3D printing of thermoplastic plastics. Around 1,700 exhibitors present polymer materials, machinery and processing technology to roughly 45,000 visitors across 9 halls.

Material exhibitors regularly present biopolymer compounds, biobased and recycled materials and energy-efficient processing solutions, making Fakuma a relevant barometer for industrial bioplastics adoption in European plastics processing.

## Topics & Focus Areas

- **Materials**: Engineering thermoplastics, biopolymer compounds, recycled materials and additives
- **Machines and Equipment**: Injection molding, extrusion, thermoforming and 3D printing technology
- **Processing Technology**: Molten-material priming, part handling, automation and Industry 4.0
- **Ancillaries**: Drying, dosing, temperature control and recycling integration
- **Sustainability**: Energy-efficient processing, circular materials and recyclable design

## Why Attend

- **Processors**: Compare the complete machine and material supplier base for European plastics processing
- **Material Producers**: Reach processors actively sourcing compounds, including biobased and recycled grades
- **Machine Builders**: Launch technology to a concentrated professional audience
- **Recyclers & Compounders**: Position recycled/biobased competencies with industrial buyers

## Typical Attendance

The fair draws roughly 45,000 professionals: plastics processors, compounders, machine builders, material suppliers, automotive and packaging industry engineers, recycling specialists and industry press.
