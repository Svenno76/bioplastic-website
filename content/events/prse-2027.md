## Event Overview

PRSE 2027 is the 11th edition of the Plastics Recycling Show Europe, the pan-European exhibition and conference dedicated to plastics recycling. Held at RAI Amsterdam (Halls 1 and 5), the event brings together recyclers, brand owners, machinery suppliers, policymakers and sustainability experts across the plastics recycling value chain.

The free-to-attend exhibition is paired with a conference covering market intelligence, regulation and practical solutions for a circular plastics economy, and the 2027 edition continues the textiles recycling feature introduced at recent editions. PRSE is organized by Crain Communications in partnership with Plastics Recyclers Europe.

## Topics & Focus Areas

- **Recycling Technologies**: Sorting, washing, grinding, and reprocessing machinery
- **Recycled Materials**: PET, PE, PP, PS, and engineering-plastics recyclates and compounds
- **Textiles Recycling**: Fiber-to-fiber recycling and circular textile value chains
- **Regulation**: EU Plastics Regulation, PPWR implementation, recyclability and recycled-content targets
- **Brand Owner Commitments**: Recycled-content sourcing strategies and design-for-recycling
- **Collection & Sorting**: EPR schemes, deposit-return systems and sorting infrastructure
- **Quality & Standards**: Recyclate quality assurance, certification and traceability

## Why Attend

- **Recyclers**: Meet the complete supplier base for plant technology and materials off-take
- **Brand Owners**: Source quality recyclates and meet PPWR recycled-content obligations
- **Machinery Suppliers**: Exhibit to Europe's largest concentrated recycling audience
- **Policy & Regulators**: Track implementation of EU circular-economy legislation
- **Investors**: Assess the rapidly consolidating European recycling sector

## Typical Attendance

The show attracts a pan-European professional audience:

- Brand owners, retailers and packaging convertors
- Machinery and equipment manufacturers
- Waste management and collection companies
- Polymer producers and distributors
