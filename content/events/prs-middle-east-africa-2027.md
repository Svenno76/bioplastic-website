## Event Overview

PRS Middle East & Africa 2027 brings the Plastics Recycling Show format to the Dubai World Trade Centre, creating a dedicated exhibition and conference for the emerging MEA plastics recycling industry. The event connects regional recyclers, waste-management companies, brand owners and policymakers with the international technology suppliers and off-takers active in the region.

The show follows the PRS model proven in Europe, Asia and — from 2027 — Brazil: a free-to-attend exhibition floor paired with a conference on circular economy strategy, technology and market conditions. It is organised by Crain Communications in partnership with Plastics Recyclers Europe.

## Topics & Focus Areas

- **Emerging Recycling Markets**: Collection infrastructure and reprocessing capacity across the GCC and Africa
- **Recyclates for Regional Industry**: Construction, packaging and industrial applications
- **Technology Transfer**: Sorting and reprocessing systems for high-growth markets
- **Policy & Vision**: UAE and Saudi circular-economy programs and plastic-diversion targets
- **Trade & Off-take**: Connecting MEA recyclate supply with regional and global demand

## Why Attend

- **Regional Recyclers**: Meet global technology suppliers in the region's most connected city
- **Brand Owners**: Develop supply chains for recycled content in fast-growing MEA markets
- **Technology Suppliers**: Establish a presence in an emerging recycling region ahead of competitors
- **Government & Policy Teams**: Benchmark circular-economy programs against international practice

## Typical Attendance

The show draws the MEA recycling ecosystem: recyclers, waste-management and municipal operators, brand owners, convertors, machinery suppliers, polymer producers, regulators from the GCC and African markets, plus international traders, consultants and investors.
