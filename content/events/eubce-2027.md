## Event Overview

EUBCE 2027 is the 35th edition of the European Biomass Conference and Exhibition, the world's leading platform for biomass energy, biorefining, and bio-based materials. After years in The Hague, the conference moves to the Centre des Congrès de Reims in Reims, France, and brings together researchers, technology providers, companies, institutions and policymakers shaping the future of biomass and bio-based solutions.

The conference combines a multi-track scientific and technical programme with an exhibition, poster sessions and networking formats, and is supported by the European Commission (JRC). Late abstract submissions are currently being accepted.

## Topics & Focus Areas

- **Biomass Valorization**: Sustainable utilization of biomass resources
- **Biorefining Technologies**: Conversion of biomass into chemicals, materials, and fuels
- **Biopolymers & Biomaterials**: Development and applications of bio-based polymers
- **Bioenergy**: Biofuels, biogas, and sustainable energy production
- **Circular Bioeconomy**: Integration of biomass into circular economy models
- **Sustainable Resource Management**: Responsible sourcing and feedstock strategies
- **Policy & Sustainability**: Regulatory frameworks and sustainability metrics
