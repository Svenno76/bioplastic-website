## Event Overview

CHINAPLAS 2027 is the 39th International Exhibition on Plastics and Rubber Industries, organized by Adsale Exhibition Services. Recognized as Asia's leading plastics and rubber trade fair, CHINAPLAS has been held since 1983 and alternates annually between Shanghai and Shenzhen. The 2027 edition returns to the Shenzhen World Exhibition & Convention Center (Bao'an) under the theme "Advancing a Smarter, Circular Future – As One".

The fair spans the entire industry chain — from plastics and rubber raw materials to machinery and end-use applications — and is a key magnet for sustainable-machinery and circular-materials solutions, including biobased and compostable plastics, recycling technologies, and high-tech materials zones. With an exhibition area of over 380,000 square meters, 8 national/regional pavilions and 17 theme zones, CHINAPLAS 2027 features more than 4,600 exhibitors from about 40 countries and regions.

## Topics & Focus Areas

- **Bioplastics & Biobased Materials**: Biobased, compostable and biodegradable polymers for packaging and durable goods
- **Recycled & Circular Materials**: Mechanical and chemical recycling technologies, recyclates and recyclable product design
- **Smart Manufacturing**: Industry 4.0 automation, robotics and digitalization for plastics processing
- **Sustainable Machinery**: Energy-efficient injection molding, extrusion and processing equipment
- **High-Tech Materials**: Engineering plastics, composites and additives for automotive, electronics and medical applications
- **Packaging Innovation**: Flexible and rigid packaging technology and materials
- **End-Use Applications**: Automotive, electronics, packaging, building & construction and consumer goods

## Why Attend

- **Material Producers**: Showcase and source biobased and recycled polymer solutions for the Asian market
- **Processors & Converters**: See the latest machinery and materials across 17 themed zones
- **Brand Owners**: Discover circular packaging and product solutions to hit sustainability targets
- **Recyclers**: Connect with technology suppliers for collection, sorting and reprocessing
- **Equipment Manufacturers**: Reach the largest gathering of Asian plastics processors
