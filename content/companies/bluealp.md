## Overview

BlueAlp is a Dutch chemical recycling technology company founded in 2014, headquartered in Eindhoven, Netherlands. The company has developed and commercialized a continuous rotary kiln pyrolysis technology that converts mixed, contaminated plastic waste into high-quality pyrolysis oil. This pyrolysis oil serves as a circular feedstock for steam crackers, enabling the production of new plastics with recycled content.

BlueAlp's technology addresses a critical gap in the circular economy: the ability to process mixed, contaminated, and multi-layer plastic waste streams that are unsuitable for mechanical recycling. Their continuous process operates at technology readiness level 9 (TRL 9), with proven commercial deployment at the Renasci facility in Oostende, Belgium (21 kt/year capacity).

## Core Technology: Continuous Rotary Kiln Pyrolysis

**Process Overview:**

BlueAlp's pyrolysis technology uses a continuous rotary kiln reactor that gradually heats mixed plastic waste in an oxygen-free environment. The process:

1. **Feedstock Preparation**: Mixed plastic waste is shredded and fed into the rotary kiln
2. **Thermal Decomposition**: Plastics are gradually heated to 400-500°C in an oxygen-free environment, breaking polymer chains into smaller hydrocarbon molecules
3. **Vapor Phase Conversion**: The resulting vapors are condensed into liquid pyrolysis oil
4. **Product Upgrading**: The pyrolysis oil can be further refined or fed directly to steam crackers

**Key Technical Advantages:**

- **Continuous Operation**: Unlike batch processes, continuous rotary kiln enables 24/7 operation with consistent product quality
- **TRL 9 Maturity**: Proven at commercial scale at Renasci (21 kt/year) since 2020
- **Flexible Feedstock**: Handles mixed, contaminated, and multi-layer plastics that mechanical recycling cannot process
- **High Liquid Yield**: Typically 70-75% liquid yield from polyolefin-rich waste streams
- **Low Contaminant Levels**: Pyrolysis oil meets steam cracker specifications after standard upgrading

## Market Position & Impact

BlueAlp occupies a unique position in the chemical recycling landscape:

- **Technology Differentiator**: Continuous rotary kiln vs. batch processes (TRL 9 vs. TRL 6-7 for most competitors)
- **Feedstock Flexibility**: Handles mixed polyolefins (PE, PP), multi-layer films, and contaminated streams
- **Circular Feedstock**: Produces pyrolysis oil that replaces fossil naphtha in steam crackers
- **Regulatory Alignment**: Enables compliance with EU recycled content mandates (PPWR) and US state-level mandates

**Environmental Impact:**
- Each tonne of plastic waste processed avoids ~2.5 tonnes CO2e vs. incineration
- Pyrolysis oil reduces fossil naphtha demand by ~70-75%
- Enables circular production of food-grade plastics via mass balance allocation

## Future Roadmap
