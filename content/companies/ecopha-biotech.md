## Overview

Ecopha Biotech is an Australian biotechnology company pioneering eco-friendly polyhydroxyalkanoate (PHA) bioplastics derived from renewable resources. Established in Malaysia in 2008 and expanded to Australia in 2013, Ecopha has become a leading innovator in fully biodegradable biopolymers for food packaging, agriculture, medical, and textile applications.

The company is known for breakthrough innovations including the world-first production of PHB from pongamia oil—a carbon-sequestering, non-edible crop—enabling scalable production of marine-safe, microplastic-free bioplastics.

### Core Technology: PHA Biopolymers

**What is PHA:**
- Polyhydroxyalkanoates—fully biodegradable biopolymers
- Synthesized from renewable resources (sugars, vegetable oils)
- Biodegrades in soil and marine environments without microplastic residue
- No industrial composting required

**Manufacturing Feedstocks:**
- Sugar-based fermentation
- Vegetable oils (previously canola, now pongamia)
- **Pongamia Oil (Innovation):** Carbon-sequestering, non-edible crop oil enabling sustainable, large-scale PHA production

### Dual-Output Biorefinery Process

Ecopha's patented process—developed with Terra Sol Studio and Australian research institutions—co-produces materials and fuels from a single feedstock:

1. **Pongamia cultivation** on marginal land → seed harvest
2. **Oil extraction** → Pongamia oil (non-edible)
3. **Fermentation** → Microbial conversion to PHA
4. **Co-product valorization** → Protein meal for animal feed, biochar for soil amendment
5. **PHA extraction & purification** → Bioplastic resin

The same pongamia feedstock also opens sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) pathways, creating a dual materials-and-fuels platform.

### Breakthrough Innovation: Pongamia-Based PHB

**World First Achievement:**
- Developed novel method to produce PHB (a type of PHA) from pongamia oil
- **Benefits:**
  - Uses non-food crop (avoids food security concerns)
  - Carbon-negative production (pongamia sequesters carbon)
  - Scalable to industrial production levels
  - Produces marine-biodegradable, microplastic-free plastic
  - PCT patent-filed technology
  - FDA and food-grade certified

### PHA Grades and Performance

- **Injection molding grades:** Rigid applications (cutlery, containers)
- **Extrusion grades:** Films, sheets, straws, coffee cup liners
- **Fiber grades:** Nonwovens, textiles, agricultural mulch films
- **Custom grades:** Tailored molecular weight, crystallinity, comonomer ratio

**Certifications and compliance:**
- Home compostable: AS 5810 / NF T 51-800
- Marine biodegradable: ASTM D6691
- Food contact: FDA / EU compliant
